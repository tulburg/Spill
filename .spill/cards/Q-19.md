S-35: deleting a spec removes all of its implementation, so Claude must know what that is. My pick is A: it costs nothing while building and stays exact.
- A. Claude records the files it touched on the spec as it builds (a "Files" list on the card), and removal works from that list.
- B. Every commit names the spec ID, and removal reverts those commits.
- C. Claude works it out at removal time from the code and the spec, and lists it in the confirmation question for you to check.

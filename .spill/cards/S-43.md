## Purpose
When you are away from the Mac, specs and questions wait unseen. An iOS-only companion app lets you approve, build, reject, answer questions and review designs from the phone, and notifies you when something new needs you while the Mac is idle.

## Requirements
1. iOS only. It shows what needs you: specs waiting for approval or Build, open questions, designs waiting for review, rejections and messages.
2. From the phone you can approve, press Build, reject with a reason, answer questions (single, multi and your own answer) and comment.
3. The design viewer works on the phone: renders, annotations, comments, accept and reject per variant (from the designs spec).
4. Notifications: when the Mac has been idle for a set time, newly drafted specs, new questions and new designs notify the phone. While you are at the Mac, nothing is sent.
5. The phone talks to the boards on your Mac; nothing about your boards goes to a service you did not choose.
6. Several projects: the app lists every project open in Spill on the Mac.

## Out of scope
- Android, iPad-specific layouts, an Apple Watch app.
- Editing spec text on the phone.

## Acceptance checks
1. With the Mac idle, a new question notifies the phone within a minute; answering it on the phone records the decision on the board.
2. Approve, Build and Reject on the phone show on the Mac board immediately.
3. A design variant can be accepted from the phone.

The boards live in .spill folders on the Mac, served by the Spill app. The phone needs a path to them, including when you are away from home.

- A. Same Wi-Fi only: the Mac app serves the phone directly on the local network (simplest, no account; nothing works away from home)
- B. A private tunnel you already use (for example Tailscale): works anywhere, still only your devices
- C. A small relay service run for Spill that both apps connect to (works anywhere without setup, but board data passes through it)
- D. iCloud: the Mac app mirrors pending items and your actions through your iCloud account

You want a way to talk to Claude that is not tied to a card, without adding another visual cluster. Drawn in Paper, board 13. Whichever you pick, a message is logged as a board-level event, wakes Claude like any approval, and Claude replies through a new MCP tool so the reply shows in the app.

Options:
- A. The top bar opens into a message box (click it or press /); replies drop under it for a few seconds
- B. A quiet "Message Claude" field at the foot of the bottom-left panel, with replies threaded above it
- C. Keyboard only: Cmd+K command bar that also searches cards; nothing on screen until called

## Purpose
When nothing is going on, the bottom-left panel shows only "Recent decisions" (and sometimes a "No update from Claude" box). It should be a summary of the whole board: what was built last, what is up next, what is waiting on you and what was decided. It can also collapse into one row of counts with an icon for each group.

## Requirements
1. The panel always shows every group that has something in it, in a fixed order: Rejected (waiting on Claude), Building / Waiting on you / No update from Claude, Spotlight (Discussing and Up next), Pending approval, Up next, Last built, Recent decisions. A group with nothing in it is left out.
2. New "Up next" group: queued specs in the order you pressed Build, up to 3. A spec already in the Spotlight's Up next is not listed again. Hidden when nothing is queued.
3. New "Last built" group: the most recently done specs, up to 3, each with how long ago it shipped.
4. Each group shows at most 3 items, with "All n" when there are more (as Pending approval and Recent decisions do now).
5. The "Hide" link becomes a chevron icon in the panel's top-right corner. It collapses the whole panel, not only the Spotlight.
6. Collapsed, the panel is one row of chips, one per group with something in it (D-13): Building, Spotlight, Pending approval, Open questions waiting on you, Rejected, Up next, Recent decisions, Last built. Each chip is the group's icon and count. Clicking the row, or the chevron, expands the panel.
7. The Building chip keeps the spec's state: a spinning icon while building, the waiting color when held by a question, and the quiet color when there has been no update.
8. The panel starts open and collapses only when the user clicks the chevron; the choice is remembered across reloads. It never opens or collapses by itself (D-14), so the spotlight moving no longer reopens it.
9. The design is drawn in Paper (file "Spill", page "Work board v2") and approved on the board before it is built.

## Out of scope
- Changing what goes in Pending approval, Spotlight or Rejected.
- Moving the panel or changing its width.

## Acceptance checks
1. On a board with no active spec and no spotlight, the panel shows Up next (if anything is queued), Last built, Pending approval (if any) and Recent decisions.
2. A queued spec that is also in the spotlight's Up next shows only in the Spotlight.
3. Clicking the chevron collapses the panel to one row of icon and count chips, one per non-empty group; the counts match the groups.
4. Reloading keeps the panel collapsed; a spotlight change does not expand it.
5. While a spec is building the collapsed row shows a spinning building chip; held by a question it shows the waiting color.

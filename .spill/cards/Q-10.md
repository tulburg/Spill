Which events should approve a spec automatically, without you pressing Approve? Pick any. You still press Build to start it.

- A. The last open question blocking it is answered.
- B. Every child spec it depends on is done.
- C. Claude reports every requirement met (its "Reqs 1-n done" covers them all).

## Purpose
Clicking any card opens its full details in a sheet docked to the right (Paper board 10A, chosen in D-2), while the canvas stays live and usable beside it.

## Requirements
1. Clicking a card (spec, question, decision) opens the side sheet on the right; the canvas shrinks to the remaining width and keeps panning and updating live. The opened card gets a selected ring.
2. Sheet header: id, kind and status, title, section, and the actions for that card: Approve and Reject for an open spec (following the open-question rule), the answer controls for an open question (S-20).
3. Tabs: Overview (the card body rendered as markdown: Purpose, Requirements, Out of scope, Acceptance checks), Links (blocked by, blocks, depends on, answers, each clickable), Activity (comments and status events, newest first, with a comment box for the user), File (the raw `.spill/cards/ID.md`).
4. Clicking a link in the sheet opens that card in the sheet and pans the canvas to it.
5. Esc or the close button closes the sheet; up and down arrows move to the previous or next card in the same column.
6. Live: if the open card changes, the sheet updates in place without losing the active tab or scroll position.
7. The open card id is kept in the URL hash, so a reload reopens it.
8. Approving or rejecting a spec from the sheet closes the sheet once the change is saved (the card updates on the canvas behind it).
9. Clicking empty canvas while the sheet is open closes it. A drag to pan does not count as a click, and clicking another card switches the sheet to that card.

## Out of scope
- Editing the card body from the sheet.
- The popup and document reader layouts (boards 10 and 10B).

## Acceptance checks
- Click a spec: the sheet opens with Overview, the canvas still pans.
- Approve from the sheet works and the card updates on the canvas.
- Links tab: clicking Q-2 opens Q-2 in the sheet.
- A Claude comment on the open card appears in Activity live.
- Esc closes it; reload with #S-3 reopens S-3.
- Approve from the sheet: the sheet closes and the card shows Approved. Same for Reject after the reason is sent.
- Click empty canvas: the sheet closes. Drag the canvas: it stays open.

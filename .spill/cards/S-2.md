## Purpose
When Claude sets a spec `active`, the user can see at a glance, on the card and in the bottom-left panel, that it is being built and how far along it is.

## Requirements
### On the card
1. An `active` card has a green border with a slow light sweep travelling around it (a "working" ring) and an animated "Claude building" label (three pulsing dots).
2. The spotlight tag on an active card reads "Building" in green, not "In discussion".
3. The card shows a thin progress bar with "n of m requirements". Steps come from the spec's numbered requirements and are checked off as Claude comments "Req n done" (or equivalent) on the card.
4. The latest Claude event on the card shows as one line under the title ("Commented: ...", "Linked ..."), and it fades in when it changes.
5. The connector from the parent to the active card is green with a moving dash (flow toward the card).

### In the bottom-left panel
6. While a spec is active, the panel's top group is "Building" instead of "Discussing": a green spinner, the card id and title, elapsed time since it went active (ticking), and the same progress bar.
7. Clicking the Building item pans to the card (same as spotlight items).
8. When the spec moves to `done`, the Building item plays a short check animation, then the group returns to Discussing.

### Rules
9. Only one card is `active` at a time (config `maxActiveSpecs`).
10. Reduced motion: the sweep, dashes, dots, spinner and shimmer become static green.

### Shimmer
11. While a spec is active, a soft light band sweeps left to right across the filled part of its progress bar on the card, and across the green Building item in the bottom-left panel, about every 2 seconds, so both read as live work.

## Out of scope
- Streaming the full Claude transcript into the card.
- The composer (S-8).

## Acceptance checks
- `spill_set_status S-n active`: ring, Building tag, and the panel's Building group appear within one second.
- A Claude comment "Req 2 done" moves the progress bar on the card and in the panel.
- `spill_set_status S-n done`: the ring stops, the panel shows the check, then returns to Discussing.
- With reduced motion on, nothing animates.
- An active spec shows the shimmer moving across its progress bar and the panel item.

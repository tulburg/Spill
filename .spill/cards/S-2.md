## Purpose
When Claude sets a spec `active`, the user can see at a glance that it is being built and how far along it is.

## Requirements
1. An `active` card has a green border with a slow moving light sweep around it (a "working" ring) and the label "Claude building".
2. The card shows a step list or progress bar (steps from the spec's requirements, checked off as Claude comments).
3. The latest Claude action shows as one line on the card ("Editing card.tsx"), updated live.
4. The connector from the parent spec to the active spec is green.
5. The collapsed composer (S-8) shows "Claude building S-n" with elapsed time; clicking it focuses the card.
6. Only one card can be `active` at a time (config `maxActiveSpecs`).
7. Motion respects reduced motion: the sweep becomes a static green ring.

## Out of scope
- Streaming the full Claude transcript into the card.

## Acceptance checks
- `spill_set_status S-n active`: ring, label and composer status appear within one second.
- `spill_set_status S-n done`: ring stops, card moves to done styling.

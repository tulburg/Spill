## Purpose
Part 3 of S-42. Review designs where they are shown: point at a spot, say what to change, and accept or reject the design (or each variant, when the spec offers several).

## Requirements
1. In the large artboard view, click a spot to drop a numbered pin and type a note; pins stay at their spot when zooming.
2. A design has a comment thread for notes that are not about one spot: one thread per variant, or one for the whole design when the spec has no variants.
3. Accept and Reject (reject with a reason, multiline like S-41) apply to each variant, or to the whole design when there are no variants. Accepting one variant does not reject the others automatically.
4. Pins, comments, accepts and rejects reach Claude's inbox with the spec, variant (if any) and pin number; MCP gives Claude the pin positions and text (spill_design_feedback).
5. Claude can reply to a pin or comment, and mark a pin resolved after changing the design.
6. A spec with designs waiting for review cannot be approved until its design (or one variant) is accepted (like open questions).
7. Rejected and accepted variants stay on the spec; nothing is deleted.

## Out of scope
- Drawing or editing designs by hand.
- Designs per spec version (S-47).

## Acceptance checks
1. A pin dropped on an artboard reaches the inbox with its number and note, and spill_design_feedback returns its position.
2. Accepting variant A and rejecting B shows both statuses on the spec; Approve stays disabled until one is accepted. A spec without variants is accepted as a whole.
3. Claude's reply and resolve on a pin show in the viewer.

## Purpose
Part 3 of S-42. Review designs where they are shown: point at a spot and say what to change. The viewer is for looking and commenting only; the design is signed off once, by answering the spec's design question (the variant to keep, or approve the design when there are no variants). Once Claude has acted on a note it resolves it, and the note leaves the review, so only open notes show and the spot is free for new ones.

## Requirements
1. In the large artboard view, Shift+click on an artboard drops a numbered pin and opens the note box, on the whole board and on a single artboard alike. A plain click never pins: on the whole board it picks a variant, on a single artboard it does nothing. Holding Shift shows the comment cursor in both views; without Shift the cursor is the normal pointer. The hints in both views say "Shift+click to pin a note". Pins stay at their spot when zooming.
2. A design has a comment thread for notes that are not about one spot: one thread per variant, or one for the whole design when the spec has no variants.
3. The viewer has no Accept, Reject or Undo accept; its review panel holds only pins, comment threads and replies.
4. Pins and comments reach Claude's inbox with the spec, variant (if any) and pin number; MCP gives Claude the pin positions and text (spill_design_feedback).
5. Claude can reply to a pin or comment, and resolve either one after changing the design for it. Only Claude resolves.
6. Design sign-off is one design question per spec (spill_ask design: true), blocking it. The app builds its options from the spec's designs; options in the body are ignored:
   - With variants: one option per variant ("A. Variant A", "B. Variant B", ...), then "Needs changes".
   - Without variants: "A. Approve this design" and "B. Needs changes".
7. Answering with a variant (or "Approve this design") approves the spec and records the chosen variant; the inbox answer names it. "Needs changes" leaves the spec unapproved; Claude reworks the design from the pins, comments and answer text, then reopens the same question.
8. The design question card has an "Open designs" link to the viewer (whole board, or the one design). While it is open, the spec's Approve is disabled and points to the question (replacing "Accept the design or a variant first").
9. Once answered, the viewer and the sheet's Designs section mark the chosen variant "Chosen", and the approved snapshot (S-47) records it. Other variants show no status. Nothing is deleted.
10. A resolved pin or comment is cleared from the UI: its pin leaves the artboard and it leaves the review panel, with its replies. Nothing in the panel lists resolved notes. (It stays in the feedback file, so an approved version's snapshot (S-47) still shows it as it was.)
11. spill_design_feedback lists only open pins and comments, plus a count of resolved ones. The MCP instructions (the server's instructions, the spill_ask, spill_design_feedback and spill_design_reply descriptions, and SPILL.md) tell Claude to work through the open notes and, for each one it acted on, reply with what changed and resolve it in the same call; and that design sign-off is the one design question, with no options listed.
12. Earlier accept and reject records in feedback.json are kept but no longer shown.

## Out of scope
- Drawing or editing designs by hand.
- Designs per spec version (S-47).
- Resolving or reopening notes by the user.
- Choosing several variants.

## Acceptance checks
1. A Shift+click pin on an artboard reaches the inbox with its number and note, in both views, and spill_design_feedback returns its position. A plain click opens no note box in either view.
2. The cursor over a design is the pointer without Shift and the comment cursor with Shift, in both views.
3. The viewer has no Accept or Reject.
4. On a board copy, a design question for a spec with variants A, B, C shows A, B, C and "Needs changes"; answering B approves the spec, the inbox names B, and the viewer and sheet mark B as chosen. A spec without variants gets "Approve this design" and "Needs changes".
5. With the design question open, the spec's Approve is disabled and points to it; "Open designs" on the question opens the viewer.
6. Claude's reply on a pin shows in the viewer; resolving a pin removes it from the artboard and the panel, resolving a comment removes it from the panel, and a new pin can be dropped on the same spot.
7. spill_design_feedback shows only open notes, plus a resolved count.

## Purpose
Part 3 of S-42. Review designs where they are shown: point at a spot, say what to change, and accept or reject the design (or each variant, when the spec offers several). Once Claude has acted on a note it resolves it, and the note leaves the review, so only open notes show and the spot is free for new ones.

## Requirements
1. In the large artboard view, drop a numbered pin on a spot and type a note: click on one artboard, Shift+click on the whole board (a plain click there picks a variant). Holding Shift shows a comment cursor. Pins stay at their spot when zooming.
2. A design has a comment thread for notes that are not about one spot: one thread per variant, or one for the whole design when the spec has no variants.
3. Accept and Reject (reject with a reason, multiline like S-41) apply to each variant, or to the whole design when there are no variants. Accepting one variant does not reject the others automatically.
4. Pins, comments, accepts and rejects reach Claude's inbox with the spec, variant (if any) and pin number; MCP gives Claude the pin positions and text (spill_design_feedback).
5. Claude can reply to a pin or comment, and resolve either one after changing the design for it. Only Claude resolves.
6. A spec with designs waiting for review cannot be approved until its design (or one variant) is accepted (like open questions).
7. Rejected and accepted variants stay on the spec; nothing is deleted.
8. A resolved pin or comment is cleared from the UI: its pin leaves the artboard and it leaves the review panel, with its replies. Nothing in the panel lists resolved notes. (It stays in the feedback file, so an approved version's snapshot (S-47) still shows it as it was.)
9. spill_design_feedback lists only open pins and comments, plus a count of resolved ones. The MCP instructions (the server's instructions, the spill_design_feedback and spill_design_reply descriptions, and SPILL.md) tell Claude to work through the open notes and, for each one it acted on, reply with what changed and resolve it in the same call.

## Out of scope
- Drawing or editing designs by hand.
- Designs per spec version (S-47).
- Resolving or reopening notes by the user.

## Acceptance checks
1. A pin dropped on an artboard reaches the inbox with its number and note, and spill_design_feedback returns its position.
2. Accepting variant A and rejecting B shows both statuses on the spec; Approve stays disabled until one is accepted. A spec without variants is accepted as a whole.
3. Claude's reply on a pin shows in the viewer.
4. Claude resolving a pin removes it from the artboard and the panel; resolving a comment removes it from the panel. A new pin can be dropped on the same spot.
5. spill_design_feedback shows only open notes, plus a resolved count.

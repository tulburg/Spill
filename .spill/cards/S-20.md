## Purpose
Answer an open question right on its canvas card, without the terminal. This is a small first step that lands before S-3. S-3 still owns the question popup and the question types (Q-2).

## Requirements
1. On an open question card, each lettered option (A, B, C) is a button with hover and pressed states.
2. Clicking an option selects it, which highlights it, and shows a confirm row under the options: "Answer B" and "Cancel". Nothing is recorded until "Answer" is clicked. Clicking another option moves the selection.
3. Under the options there is an "Other" text field. Typing in it clears any option selection, and Enter or "Answer" submits the text.
4. A question with no options shows only the text field.
5. Answering does exactly what `spill answer Q-n text` does: it records a `D-n` decision by the user (titled with the option letter and text, or the typed text), sets the question to `answered`, and unblocks its specs when nothing else blocks them. It shows up in Claude's `spill_inbox`.
6. Once answered, the card shows the chosen answer highlighted and its options are no longer clickable.
7. A selection or typed text survives live board updates (the redraw does not wipe it).

## Out of scope
- The question popup, the queue and Accept or Reject proposals (S-3).
- Multi select (Q-2).
- Changing an answer after it is recorded.

## Acceptance checks
- On a board copy, click B on a question, then Answer: a `D-n` appears, the question is `answered`, and `spill_inbox` reports it.
- Click an option and then Cancel: nothing is recorded.
- Type text in Other and press Enter: a `D-n` with that text is recorded.
- A live change from another card while an option is selected does not lose the selection.

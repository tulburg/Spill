## Purpose
Every status has one distinct, consistent look so the canvas is readable at a glance.

## Requirements
1. **Draft**: dashed status ring, neutral border, muted text. Shows "Draft by Claude" or "Draft by you".
2. **Discussing**: amber quarter ring, comment count in the header.
3. **Awaiting approval**: amber border, Approve and Reject buttons on the card.
4. **Rejected**: kept on the board, faded, "Rejected" label and reason; not deleted.
5. **Done**: filled grey check, faded card, sorted visually out of the way.
6. A legend in the canvas corner maps each look to its status.
7. Status colors: green only for approved and building, amber only for things that need the user.

## Out of scope
- User configurable status colors.

## Acceptance checks
- A board with one card in every status: each is identifiable without reading the label.

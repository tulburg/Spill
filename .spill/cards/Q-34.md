Real push notifications on iOS need Apple's push service (APNs), a paid Apple Developer account, and a sender with the app's push key. Without that, the app can only check in the background now and then, which iOS limits to roughly every 15 minutes or more.

- A. Real push: the Mac app sends through APNs directly with your developer key (needs the paid account)
- B. Real push through the relay from the connection question (needs the relay and the paid account)
- C. No push for now: background refresh plus a check whenever the app opens

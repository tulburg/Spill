## Purpose
Cards fade for different reasons and the rules grew one fix at a time, so live work (like queued specs) ends up faded. One written rule set, built in one place, so a faded card always means the same thing.

## Requirements
1. Two kinds of fade, never mixed: a status fade says the card is finished or out of play; an attention fade says "look elsewhere for now".
2. Status fade applies to done, rejected, replaced, answered and dropped cards, and to decisions (a decision is a finished record). The summary panel and search do not fade.
3. Attention fade never applies to live work or to anything that needs the user: active, approved, queued and discussing specs and open questions always stay at full strength.
4. The spotlight fades only finished cards (including decisions) and drafts outside it (D-29).
5. Hovering or selecting any faded card shows it at full strength; the focus fade from a link chip stays as it is.
6. The rules are written in SPILL.md and implemented as one block of CSS, so new card states have to pick a side.
7. On an answered question, the chosen answer is muted like the rest of the card; its green ring alone marks it.

## Out of scope
- Changing the spotlight itself (what Claude puts in it).
- Colors of the statuses.

## Acceptance checks
1. With a spotlight set, active, approved, queued and discussing specs and open questions are all at full strength.
2. Done, rejected, replaced, answered and dropped cards and decisions show their status fade, and full strength on hover and selection.
3. An answered question's chosen answer has muted text with its green ring kept.
4. The fade rules in the docs match the CSS.

## Purpose
Building uses Spill's primary color (the ink of the logo and the Answer and Approve buttons) with a shimmer, so green means only approved and done. Per D-10.

## Requirements
1. The active card's sweeping ring goes from green to ink (#18181B) with a light glint traveling around it; the outer halo is a faint ink tint.
2. The "Building" spot tag, the spinner, "Claude building" and its dots, the latest-action arrow and the progress numbers use ink.
3. The progress bar fill is ink with the existing shimmer band moving across it, on the card and in the panel.
4. The panel's Building group and its item use ink text and border instead of green; the shimmer stays.
5. The animated dashed connector lines into an active card ("from the stem") are ink.
6. The done moment (check, Shipped, "n of n built") stays green.
7. Reduced motion: static ink ring and lines, no shimmer.

## Out of scope
- Any layout change; only colors.
- Approved, done and checks colors.

## Acceptance checks
- An active spec shows an ink ring with a moving glint, ink tag, spinner, dots and progress.
- The panel item is ink with the shimmer.
- Connector lines into the active card are ink and animated.
- Marking it done shows the green check as before.
- No green remains on an active card or its panel item.

## Purpose
The rejection reason is a one-line input squeezed next to the Reject button, so longer reasons are cramped and cannot have line breaks.

## Requirements
1. Pressing Reject on a card (canvas or sheet) opens a textarea in place of the action row, three lines tall to start, full card width.
2. The textarea grows with the text up to 200px, then scrolls; it never shrinks below three lines.
3. The Reject button sits below the textarea, right-aligned, and stays disabled until the reason has text.
4. Enter adds a new line; Cmd+Enter (Ctrl+Enter) sends; Esc cancels and restores the action row.
5. Line breaks in the reason are kept: they show in the rejection queue, the card's activity and spill_rejections.
6. Styling reuses the existing comment textarea (same radius, padding, type) with the red focus ring rejection uses today; no new design.

## Out of scope
- Rejecting without a reason.
- The comment and update boxes, which already grow.

## Acceptance checks
1. Reject opens a three-line textarea with the Reject button below it.
2. Typing past three lines grows the box; past 200px it scrolls.
3. Enter makes a new line; Cmd+Enter rejects; Esc cancels.
4. A two-line reason arrives with its line break in spill_rejections and the card's activity.

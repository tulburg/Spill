S-35: when you confirm deleting a done spec and replacing it, Claude removes its code. This decides what the board keeps. My pick is A: the history of why it was built and replaced stays readable.
- A. The card stays as "Replaced", dimmed, linked to its replacement, with its questions and decisions kept.
- B. The card and its questions and decisions are removed from the board; only the replacement remains.
- C. The card is removed, but its decisions move to the replacement spec.

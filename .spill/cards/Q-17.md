D-17 makes S-32 much larger than one buildable piece: a change-request flow, spec versions with a diff view, and replacing a spec (including deleting its code). I recommend splitting it. S-32 would become the overview, and each part would own its own questions and decisions (D-16 goes to part 1, D-17 stays on the overview).
1. Ask for a change on a done spec: the comment box is the main action (Revert removed, D-16); the request reaches Claude as a change request; the card shows "Change requested"; Claude may move the spec back to active, ask questions on it, or update it back to awaiting approval.
2. Spec versions and diff: every body update saves a version, and the sheet shows what changed since the last approved version.
3. Replace a spec: when a change is too big, Claude branches a new spec or proposes deleting the spec and replacing it, where deleting means removing all of its implementation; you confirm before anything is deleted.
- A. Split into these three
- B. Keep S-32 as one spec
- C. Split it differently (say how)

When the bottom-left panel is collapsed (S-31), it becomes one row of chips. Each chip is an icon plus a count, and a chip only appears when its count is above zero. Which groups get a chip? My pick is A to E: they are things that are moving or waiting on someone. Decisions and Last built only grow, so a count of them says little.
- A. Building (the active spec, colored by building, waiting or quiet)
- B. Spotlight (cards being discussed and up next)
- C. Pending approval
- D. Open questions waiting on you
- E. Rejected, waiting on Claude
- F. Up next (queued specs)
- G. Recent decisions
- H. Last built

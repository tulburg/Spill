## Purpose
Specs waiting on your approval are easy to lose: child specs ship and questions get answered while the parent spec still sits in draft or discussing. A "Pending approval" group in the bottom-left panel lists them so nothing waits unseen.

## Requirements
1. A "Pending approval" group shows in the bottom-left panel, above Recent decisions, whenever at least one spec is in `draft` or `discussing`. Hidden when there are none.
2. Header: "Pending approval" and a count ("All 4").
3. Up to 3 rows, same row style as Recent decisions: spec ID, title (truncated), and how long it has waited ("2h").
4. Order: specs with nothing blocking them first (ready to approve), then specs waiting on open questions; oldest first within each.
5. A spec waiting on open questions shows a small lock and "Q-n" in place of the age.
6. Hovering a row highlights the spec on the canvas; clicking pans to it, like Recent decisions.
7. Approving or rejecting a spec removes it from the group live.

## Out of scope
- Approving from the panel (approve on the card or in the sheet).
- Auto-approving a parent when its children are done.

## Acceptance checks
- With 4 draft or discussing specs: group shows "All 4" and 3 rows, unblocked first.
- A spec blocked by an open question shows the lock and its Q-n.
- Approving one on a board copy drops it from the group and the count.
- No draft or discussing specs: the group is hidden.

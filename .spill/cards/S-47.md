## Purpose
Part 4 of S-42. A spec's approved versions (S-34) should keep the designs that were approved with them, so history shows what was approved and why.

## Requirements
1. When a spec is approved, the approved version records its designs at that moment: artboards, their HTML, statuses, pins and comments, stored once per distinct state under .spill (D-35). Text edits keep recording text versions without designs.
2. Comparing with an approved version in the sheet shows that version's designs (a Now / version switch), read only, with their statuses and pins as they were. Opening one in the larger canvas is read only too, with a way back to the current design.
3. The version diff notes design changes since the compared approved version: variants added or removed, artboards edited, added or removed, and status changes (for example Waiting -> Accepted).
4. Editing a design after approval does not change the approved version's snapshot.

## Out of scope
- A visual diff between two renders.
- Design snapshots for versions that were never approved.

## Acceptance checks
1. After approval, an edited design leaves the approved version showing the design as it was approved.
2. The diff against an approved version lists the design changes.
3. Snapshots survive a restart and are stored under .spill.

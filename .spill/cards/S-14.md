## Purpose
Blocking relationships cross the hierarchy, so they are not drawn as lines (S-9 lines are parent to child only). A chip says what a card waits on and points at it on demand.

## Requirements
1. A card that is blocked shows a chip "Waits on S-n" (or Q-n) in its header area.
2. Hovering the chip highlights the blocking card with an indigo ring and dims the rest; no line is drawn.
3. Clicking the chip pans to the blocking card.
4. The blocking card shows a small "Blocks 2" count; hovering it highlights the cards it blocks.
5. When the blocker is approved, done or answered, the chip disappears and the blocked card gets a "Ready" pill once.

## Out of scope
- Editing dependencies from the canvas.

## Acceptance checks
- S-3 waiting on S-5 shows the chip; hovering it rings S-5 and nothing else.

## Purpose
The canvas is the whole app. Chrome is limited to one floating toolbar at the top center and the composer at the bottom center.

## Requirements
1. No left sidebar and no right rail. The canvas fills the window.
2. A floating toolbar sits top center with two parts:
   - **Project menu** (left): project name and a dropdown with project actions (switch project, sections, decisions log, settings, open `.spill` folder).
   - **Search** (center): "Search specs" field that opens a dropdown of specs, questions and decisions. Typing filters by ID or title. Choosing a result pans the canvas to that card and selects it.
3. The search dropdown has quick filters: Needs you, Building, Open questions, Drafts. "Needs you" shows its count.
4. The toolbar shows no board summary (no spec counts, no "edited" time) and no "Ask a question" button.
5. Toasts (S-1) appear just under the toolbar.
6. `Cmd+K` focuses search.

## Out of scope
- Multiple windows or tabs per project.

## Acceptance checks
- A fresh window shows only the canvas, the toolbar and the composer.
- Typing "S-5" in search and pressing Enter focuses S-5.

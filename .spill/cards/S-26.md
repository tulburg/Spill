## Purpose
When none of a question's options fit, the answer steers the question instead of becoming a decision (D-8, D-9 were really redirects).

## Requirements
1. On a question with options, an answer that picks none of them (Other only) does not record a `D-n`. It adds a user comment "Redirect: <text>" and the question stays `open`.
2. The question card shows "Redirected: <text>" with "Claude is rewriting" until Claude updates the question; the answer controls are hidden meanwhile.
3. `spill_inbox` shows it as a `redirect` line, so Claude rewrites the same question (spill_update) and comments what changed.
4. Once Claude updates the question body, the note clears and the new options are answerable.
5. Questions without options keep today's behavior: the free answer records a decision.
6. `spill answer Q-n text` follows the same rule.

## Out of scope
- Cleaning up D-8 and D-9 (they stay as history).

## Acceptance checks
- Other on an option question: no new D-n, question open, card shows the redirect note.
- spill_inbox lists the redirect.
- spill_update on the question clears the note and shows the new options.
- A free answer on a question with no options still records a D-n.

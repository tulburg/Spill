## Purpose
A card on the canvas should answer "what is this, why does it matter, where is it, and what does it need" without opening it. Modeled on real specs (Coral research docs): each has a reason it is next, a spec section, decided rules, outputs, checks, design links and the decision that approved it.

## Requirements
1. **Header:** status icon, ID, spec section chip (for example "10.7"), status label.
2. **Title** (one line) and **why line**: the first sentence of the spec's "Why this is next" or Purpose, at most 2 lines.
3. **Facts block:** up to 3 key and value rows chosen by status:
   - Draft or discussing: requirements count, what blocks it, design needed or attached.
   - Building: step progress (n of m) and Claude's current action.
   - Approved or done: what it produced (for example "34 cards"), rules decided, check script.
4. **Branch chips:** open questions (amber), decisions, child specs, each with a count. Clicking a chip focuses that group in the Branches column (S-9).
5. **Footer:** provenance in one line ("Approved as D-045, 2d ago", "Drafted by Claude, 2h ago", "Reverted by you, 1h ago") and acceptance checks passed ("4 of 4 checks").
6. **Design link:** when the spec links Paper or Figma boards, a small "3 boards" link shows in the footer.
7. Fixed card width (column width). Height follows content, capped so the why line never exceeds 2 lines.

## Out of scope
- Editing card content on the canvas (done in the preview, S-3).

## Acceptance checks
- Every status in S-6 renders with the facts listed for it.
- A spec with 2 open questions shows an amber "2 open" chip and is in the Needs you filter.

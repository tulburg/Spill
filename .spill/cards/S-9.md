## Purpose
The canvas reads left to right like an outline: top level specs on the left, everything they produced to their right, all on straight columns.

## Requirements
1. Column 1 holds top level specs, aligned on one left edge with equal spacing.
2. Column 2 holds what each spec produced: questions, decisions and child specs. Each spec's items form a group with a small title ("From S-2: 1 question, 1 child spec"), top aligned with that spec.
3. Column 3 holds the next level: what branches produced (for example a spec blocked by a question), aligned with the item it came from.
4. Columns are hierarchy depth, never status. Status shows on each card (approved, needs you, building, and so on), per S-6.
5. Colored connector lines run from each spec to every item it produced (column 1 to 2, and column 2 to 3), like Board C. A line takes the child's state color: green for building or certified, teal for decisions, amber for needs you, indigo for new, grey for everything else. Lines sit behind cards. No lines between top level specs, and no background panels behind groups.
6. Each column has a title at the top ("Specs", "Branches", "Waiting").
7. Spacing: 24px between rows, 8px between items in a group, fixed column widths.

## Out of scope
- Free placement or dragging cards out of their column.

## Acceptance checks
- A board with 4 top level specs and their branches has every card on one of 3 straight left edges, with no panels, and every branch item has one colored line back to its parent.

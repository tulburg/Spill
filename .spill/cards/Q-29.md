When Claude sets a spotlight (or a question holds the active spec), today every card outside it drops to 45%, which is how your queued S-38 and S-39 ended up faded. S-40 keeps live work and anything that needs you at full strength either way; this picks what happens to the rest.

- A. Fade only finished cards (done, answered, rejected, replaced) and drafts not in the spotlight; everything else stays full strength
- B. Fade everything outside the spotlight except live work and things that need you (today's rule plus the S-40 exemptions)
- C. No spotlight fade at all; the spotlight only adds its ring and tag, and only status fades remain

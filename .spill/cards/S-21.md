## Purpose
A spec cannot be approved while a question on it is still open, so nothing gets approved on an unanswered assumption.

## Requirements
1. A spec with any open question linked as `blocked_by` cannot move to `approved`, from any surface (app, CLI, MCP).
2. Spill rejects the attempt with a message naming the questions: "S-3 has open questions: Q-2. Answer them first."
3. On the card, Approve is disabled while questions are open, with a short line in its place: "Answer Q-2 to approve". Clicking the question id pans to that question.
4. Reject stays available: the user can always reject a spec with open questions.
5. When the last open question is answered, Approve becomes available again on the next live update, without a reload.
6. Dropped questions do not block.

## Out of scope
- Questions that are only commented on a card, not linked as `blocked_by`.

## Acceptance checks
- `spill approve S-3` while Q-2 is open fails with the message; S-3 stays in its status.
- `POST /api/cards/S-3/approve` while Q-2 is open returns 400 with the same message.
- The S-3 card shows Approve disabled and "Answer Q-2 to approve"; Reject works.
- Answer Q-2 on its card: S-3's Approve enables live.

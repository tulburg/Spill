## Purpose
The corner button stays the one place to talk to Claude, and past conversations stay within reach as isolated floating cards, one per thread. Nothing wraps them: no panel, and the message box is its own card too.

## Requirements
1. The moment you send, the ring around the button spins, before the server confirms anything. It keeps spinning until Claude replies or the message is otherwise settled (S-8 states).
2. A thread is a message from you plus Claude's replies and any follow-ups you send into it. Each message records which thread it belongs to.
3. Clicking the button opens two kinds of isolated cards, stacked above it with clear space between them and nothing behind them:
   - the **new message box**, its own card closest to the button, with a "New message" placeholder;
   - your **recent threads**, one floating card each, newest nearest the box.
   The missed-replies list inside the box (S-8) goes away; missed replies show in their thread cards instead.
4. A thread card is one to one, with no names: your message is a small, faint caption row (with attachment thumbnails), and Claude's response is the main content: larger, full-contrast text in its own rounded message box inset inside the card (not full width). A follow-up repeats the pattern: a title row, then its response box. Each response is a single row.
5. Clicking a row expands it to its full text; clicking again collapses it.
6. Hovering a thread card shows a **Reply** button. Clicking it opens a reply box inside that card (same attachments and Enter to send as the new message box). The reply goes into that thread, even if newer threads exist.
7. A thread still in progress shows a small "Working" row with a spinner as its last row; the button ring also spins.
8. Up to 3 thread cards show at once; a "Show older" link above them reveals the rest, 3 at a time.
9. Claude side: inbox message lines say which thread they belong to ("in thread of <first message time>"), and `spill_reply` answers the latest open thread by default or a given thread.
10. Esc, or clicking away with nothing typed, closes all the cards.

## Out of scope
- Searching or deleting messages.
- Threads tied to cards.

## Acceptance checks
- Send a message: the ring spins at once, before the inbox is read.
- Open the button with 2 finished threads and 1 in progress: the new message box and 3 separate thread cards with space between, no wrapping panel; the in-progress one has a Working row.
- Hover an older thread, press Reply, send: the reply lands in that thread and `spill_inbox` names it.
- A long reply shows as one row and expands to full text on click.

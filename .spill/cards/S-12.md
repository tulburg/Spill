## Purpose
Recent decisions are easy to lose once their question closes. A thin strip keeps the latest D-n visible and shows what each one changed.

## Requirements
1. A strip at the bottom left of the canvas lists the 3 most recent decisions as small cards: ID, short title, age.
2. Hovering a decision highlights the specs it changed on the canvas; other cards dim.
3. Clicking a decision opens it in the preview (S-3 requirement 8) with "Changed: S-n, S-n".
4. "All decisions" at the end of the strip opens the decisions log from the project menu (S-7).
5. The strip stays clear of the bottom-center slot (composer or question popup, D-1).

## Out of scope
- Editing or reversing a decision from the strip.

## Acceptance checks
- After answering a question, the new D-n appears first in the strip within one second.

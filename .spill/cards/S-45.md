## Purpose
Part 2 of S-42. Show a spec's designs inside its Overview, rendered as they are, with a larger canvas for looking closely. Not every spec has variants; when it does, they are compared on the whole board, not in the sheet. The larger canvas is a window of its own, so it can sit beside the board, be resized, or go on another screen.

## Requirements
1. The side sheet's Overview gets a Designs section (after the spec text) when the spec has designs, with a count. There is no separate Designs tab.
2. The section is a single row of previews, scrolling sideways when it overflows.
3. Without variants: one preview per artboard, each with an expand button that opens that artboard in the larger canvas.
4. With variants (two or more): one preview per variant (its first artboard, the variant letter, name and artboard count). Clicking one, or the section's "Open board" button, opens the whole board: every artboard side by side, grouped by variant.
5. Each preview renders isolated (a shadow root; scripts, frames and inline handlers are stripped, so nothing runs) so its CSS cannot touch Spill's and Spill's cannot touch it.
6. The larger canvas opens in a separate window per spec, titled "S-n designs, <project>", not a modal over the board. Opening the same spec's designs again focuses its window and switches it to the requested view (whole board or one artboard); another spec opens its own window. The board stays usable while it is open.
7. In the desktop app it is a native window of the same app: it closes with its project window and shows in the Window menu. From `spill serve` in a browser it opens at its own URL in a new tab or window.
8. The canvas has zoom (fit, 100%, in and out), pan, arrow keys to step through artboards, and Esc to close the window. On the whole board, zoom and pan move the whole set, and double-clicking an artboard focuses it. An unsent note is kept when the window closes.
9. The spec card on the canvas shows a small "n designs" chip that opens the sheet at the Designs section.
10. Previews and the design window update live when Claude edits a design.
11. Works in light and dark themes (the frame keeps the design's own colors).

## Out of scope
- Pins, comments and the design question (S-46). Version history (S-47).
- Remembering design window positions between launches.

## Acceptance checks
1. A spec without variants shows one row of artboard previews in its Overview, each rendered correctly; expand opens it in the design window.
2. A spec with two variants shows one preview per variant in the row; clicking one opens the whole board with the variants side by side.
3. A design's CSS does not change Spill's styles.
4. In the desktop app, opening designs opens a second native window and the board stays interactive; opening the same spec again focuses it, another spec opens a new one, and closing the project window closes them.
5. From `spill serve` in a browser, the designs open at their own URL in a new tab or window.
6. Zoom, pan, arrow keys and Esc work in the design window.
7. An edit through MCP shows in the open sheet and design window without a reload.

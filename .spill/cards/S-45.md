## Purpose
Part 2 of S-42. Show a spec's designs inside its Overview, rendered as they are, with a larger canvas for looking closely. Not every spec has variants; when it does, they are compared on the whole board, not in the sheet.

## Requirements
1. The side sheet's Overview gets a Designs section (after the spec text) when the spec has designs, with a count. There is no separate Designs tab.
2. The section is a single row of previews, scrolling sideways when it overflows.
3. Without variants: one preview per artboard, each with an expand button that opens that artboard in the larger canvas.
4. With variants (two or more): one preview per variant (its first artboard, the variant letter, name, status and artboard count). Clicking one, or the section's "Open board" button, opens the whole board: every artboard side by side, grouped by variant.
5. Each preview renders isolated (a shadow root; scripts, frames and inline handlers are stripped, so nothing runs) so its CSS cannot touch Spill's and Spill's cannot touch it.
6. The larger canvas sits over the board, with zoom (fit, 100%, in and out), pan, arrow keys to step through artboards, and Esc to close. On the whole board, zoom and pan move the whole set, and double-clicking an artboard focuses it.
7. The spec card on the canvas shows a small "n designs" chip that opens the sheet at the Designs section.
8. Previews and the larger canvas update live when Claude edits a design.
9. Works in light and dark themes (the frame keeps the design's own colors).

## Out of scope
- Annotations, comments, accept and reject (S-46). Version history (S-47).

## Acceptance checks
1. A spec without variants shows one row of artboard previews in its Overview, each rendered correctly; expand opens it in the larger canvas.
2. A spec with two variants shows one preview per variant in the row; clicking one opens the whole board with the variants side by side.
3. A design's CSS does not change Spill's styles.
4. Zoom, pan, arrow keys and Esc work in the larger canvas.
5. An edit through MCP shows in the open sheet and canvas without a reload.

Left: review happens in the larger canvas. A panel on the right holds the verdict (Accept, Reject) and one thread: numbered pins and comments on the design. Click the artboard to drop a pin; a small box takes the note (Enter pins it, Esc cancels). Pins stay on their spot at any zoom. Claude's replies sit under each note, and a pin Claude resolved shows struck through and grey. The comment box at the bottom is for notes that are not about one spot.
Right, top: with variants, click a variant on the whole board to review it. Each variant shows its own status (Accepted, Waiting, Rejected), and the panel works on the selected one. Reject opens a multiline reason box (Cmd Enter sends).
Right, bottom: on the spec, Approve stays disabled until the design or one variant is accepted, with a line saying why.

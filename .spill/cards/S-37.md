## Purpose
Spill needs a landing page. Explore four directions (three light, one dark) as working pages, each with its own scroll motion, so one can be picked and finished.

## Requirements
1. A new `landing/` folder holds a Vite + React + TypeScript app. The root package.json lists it as a workspace (`workspaces: ["landing"]`), making the repo a monorepo; the app, CLI and tests stay where they are.
2. The four versions are each a full landing page at its own route: `/light-1`, `/light-2`, `/light-3`, `/dark`. An index at `/` links to all four.
3. Each version is designed in OpenDraw first, through its MCP server (one artboard per version), then built to match.
4. Every version follows the landing-page structure: hero (headline, subheadline, one primary CTA, a proof line, a product visual), problem to solution, 3 to 5 benefits, how it works in 3 steps, a tagline reveal section (words go from muted to full color one at a time as you scroll), FAQ, and a final CTA matching the hero.
5. Every version has its own scroll motion, beyond the shared section reveals:
   - Light 1: the hero board screenshot tilts flat and scales up as you scroll.
   - Light 2: a pinned section where cards change status (draft, approved, building, done) as you scroll through it.
   - Light 3: a horizontal scroll track walking through Ask, Approve, Build.
   - Dark: parallax layers of cards, with the building shimmer from the app.
6. All motion uses IntersectionObserver or a requestAnimationFrame-throttled scroll handler, animates only transform and opacity, and falls back to plain fades under prefers-reduced-motion.
7. Visuals use the Spill logo, Geist, and real Spill screenshots. Copy describes what Spill actually does; no invented stats, prices or testimonials (marked placeholders instead).
8. Each version works at 390px wide with no horizontal overflow.

## Out of scope
- Choosing the final version, a real domain or hosting, analytics, a working signup backend.
- Changing the Spill app itself.

## Acceptance checks
1. `bun install` and `bun run dev` in `landing/` serve all five routes.
2. Each version shows its scroll motion in the browser (screenshots at several scroll positions).
3. Each version has no horizontal overflow at 390px.
4. The tagline reveal colors its words one at a time while scrolling, and shows the final text at once under reduced motion.
5. The four OpenDraw artboards exist and each built page matches its artboard.

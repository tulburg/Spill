S-31 makes the bottom-left panel collapsible, and Spill remembers the choice across reloads. This decides what a board opened for the first time shows, and whether the panel opens by itself when something needs you.
- A. Open at first; after that, remember the choice. It opens by itself when the spotlight moves.
- B. Open at first; remember the choice. It opens by itself when the spotlight moves, and also when a spec starts building or a question starts holding the active spec.
- C. Collapsed at first; remember the choice. It never opens by itself.

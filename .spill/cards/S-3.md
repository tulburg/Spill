## Purpose
Questions that block a spec are impossible to miss and quick to answer.

## Requirements
1. A spec with open questions shows a count badge ("2 questions") in its header. The badge is the amber accent.
2. The search dropdown (S-7) shows the open question count under its "Open questions" filter.
3. A new question raises a popup that replaces the composer (S-8) in the bottom center slot until it is answered or set aside (D-1). It shows the question, the spec it belongs to, and the answer controls. "Later" sets it aside, brings the composer back, and it can be reopened from the badge.
4. Several open questions queue in the popup ("1 of 3") with next and previous.
5. The popup has two clearly different layouts:
   - **Choose one of several options**: a list of lettered options (A, B, C) with one selectable, plus "Other" for free text.
   - **Confirm a single proposal**: one proposed answer with Accept and Reject buttons, plus "Reply" for free text.
6. Answering records a `D-n` decision, closes the question, removes it from counts, and unblocks the spec if nothing else blocks it.
7. Questions also render on the canvas as cards linked to their spec.

## Preview popup
8. Clicking a card opens a preview popup over the canvas (modal or panel per Q-19) with the full spec as a document:
   - Title, status, section and provenance.
   - Why this is next, Requirements (numbered, each with its check state), Decisions (decided and alternative not taken), Open questions (answerable inline), Out of scope, Acceptance checks, Design links.
   - A properties column: status, section, parent, children, created, approved by, files touched.
   - Approve and Reject for the user; Claude can never approve.
   - Esc or clicking the canvas closes it; arrows move to the previous or next spec in the column.

## Out of scope
- Multi-user voting on answers.

## Acceptance checks
- `spill_ask` with options: popup appears with lettered list; picking one creates a decision.
- `spill_ask` with one proposal: popup shows Accept and Reject.

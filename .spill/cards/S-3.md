## Purpose
Questions that block a spec are impossible to miss and quick to answer, whether they need one choice or several.

## Requirements
1. A spec with open questions shows a count badge ("2 questions") in its header, in the amber accent.
2. The search dropdown (S-7) shows the open question count under its "Open questions" filter.
3. A question is either **single select** (pick one) or **multi select** (pick any number). Per D-6.
4. `spill_ask` takes `multi: true` for multi select; the default is single select. The card file records it so the app and the CLI agree.
5. Single select answers as today (S-20): click an option, then "Answer X". Other is free text.
6. Multi select shows checkboxes instead of option buttons; the answer button reads "Answer A, C" and is disabled until at least one is picked. Other is free text and can be combined with picks.
7. A multi select answer records one `D-n` whose title lists every pick ("A, C. title A; title C").
8. `spill answer Q-n A,C` answers a multi select question from the CLI.
9. Answering closes the question, removes it from counts, and unblocks the spec if nothing else blocks it (S-21 gate).

## Out of scope
- The bottom center popup and the preview popup from the earlier draft: replaced by in-card answering (D-2, S-20) and the side sheet (S-22).
- Multi-user voting on answers.

## Acceptance checks
- `spill_ask` without `multi`: card shows option buttons; picking one creates a decision.
- `spill_ask` with `multi: true`: card shows checkboxes; picking A and C creates one decision listing both.
- Answer button is disabled with nothing picked.
- `spill answer Q-n A,C` records the same decision as the app.
- Badge count drops when a question is answered.

## Purpose
Show the project's spec structure on the canvas without lanes: specs in column 1 are grouped under the section they belong to.

## Requirements
1. Column 1 shows a header above each group of specs that share a section: section ID, name, spec count (for example "SEC-2 MCP server, 1 spec").
2. Groups follow section order; specs inside a group keep their ID order.
3. A header shows a needs-you count in amber when any spec in the section needs the user.
4. Clicking a header collapses its specs (and their branches) to the header row; clicking again expands.
5. Headers are text on the canvas, no background or panel (S-9).

## Out of scope
- Creating or renaming sections from the canvas (done in the project menu, S-7).

## Acceptance checks
- A board with specs in 2 sections shows 2 headers, each followed only by its own specs.

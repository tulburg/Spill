## Purpose
Items in the Branches and Next level columns were single-row cards with only an ID and title. Each kind needs enough detail to act on without opening it, and decisions need an identity of their own.

## Requirements
1. **Child specs** use the full spec card from S-10 (header, title, why line, facts or reason, footer), at the column width.
2. **Decisions** have their own look, distinct from specs and questions: a teal seal icon, a "Decision" band with the ID, the decision as a statement, then "Chosen" and "Not taken" rows, and a footer with who decided, the question it answered and the specs it changed.
3. **Questions** show the question, its type (pick one or yes or no), the options or Claude's single proposal, Claude's recommendation, who asked and when, and what the question blocks.
4. Status colors stay as in S-6: amber for a question that needs the user, indigo for new, grey for done or blocked. Teal belongs to decisions only.
5. Connector lines to decisions are teal (S-9).

## Out of scope
- Answering a question from the branch card; the popup (S-3) and the preview handle that.

## Acceptance checks
- A decision card is identifiable as a decision at a glance, from color and shape, without reading its ID.

## Purpose
An approved spec reads as signed off, distinct from every other state.

## Requirements
1. An `approved` card is green: green tinted surface, green border, and a "Approved" seal (check in a badge).
2. The card has a distinct shimmer: a soft iridescent sheen that sweeps across the card once when it is approved, then rests as a subtle static sheen.
3. Approval shows who approved and when in the card footer ("Approved by you, 2m ago").
4. Only the user can approve (from the card, the detail modal, or the "Needs you" filter in search). Claude cannot.
5. Reduced motion: no sweep, static sheen only.

## Out of scope
- Signed or cryptographic approval.

## Acceptance checks
- Approve a spec: one sweep plays, card rests green with the seal.
- Approved, active and done cards are distinguishable in a grayscale screenshot by seal and border.

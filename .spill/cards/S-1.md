## Purpose
When a spec is added to the board (by Claude or the user), the user finds out without having to scan the canvas, in the app and, when Spill is in the background, from the OS (D-5).

## Requirements
1. Adding a spec shows a notice in the slot under the top bar (where Claude's replies drop, S-23): "S-n added", the title, and who added it. It stays 8 seconds.
2. The notice has a "Show" action that pans the canvas to the new card and opens it in the side sheet (S-22).
3. The new card plays a short entry highlight (scale and glow, under 600ms) and keeps its "New" pill until the user opens or hovers it.
4. The top bar's "need you" count updates with the new spec. (The search dropdown, S-7, is not built.)
5. Several specs added within 3 seconds collapse into one notice ("3 specs added"), whose Show opens the first.
6. When the Spill window is in the background (hidden or not focused), a new spec also raises a macOS notification with the same text. It is sent by the local server with the system's own `osascript`, so no new dependency is added. Other platforms get the in-app notice only.
7. Specs that already existed when the app opened do not notify.

## Out of scope
- Email or push to other devices.
- Notifications for questions, decisions, approvals (only new specs).

## Acceptance checks
- Draft a spec through MCP with the app focused: the notice appears under the top bar, Show opens the card in the sheet.
- Draft three specs quickly: one grouped notice.
- Draft a spec with the app in the background: a macOS notification appears.
- Reopen the app: no notices for existing specs.

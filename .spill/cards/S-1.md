## Purpose
When a spec is added to the board (by Claude or the user), the user finds out without having to scan the canvas.

## Requirements
1. Adding a spec shows a toast: "S-n added", the title, and who added it (Claude or user).
2. The toast has a "Show" action that pans the canvas to the new card and selects it.
3. The new card plays a short entry highlight (scale and glow, under 600ms) and keeps a "New" dot until the user opens or hovers it.
4. The "Needs you" count in the search dropdown (S-7) updates and the spec is listed under its filter.
5. Several specs added within 3 seconds collapse into one toast ("3 specs added").

## Out of scope
- Email or push to other devices.

## Acceptance checks
- Draft a spec through MCP: toast appears under the toolbar, "Show" focuses the card.
- Draft three specs quickly: one grouped toast.

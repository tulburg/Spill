## Purpose
Approval and building are two different moments. A spec can become approved in several ways (you approve it, or its open points get settled), but nothing is built until you press Build. Reject stays available the whole time.

## Requirements
1. New status `queued` between `approved` and `active`: draft -> discussing -> approved -> queued -> active -> done. Only the user moves a spec to `queued`.
2. A spec becomes `approved` either by you (Approve) or automatically (D-11) when any of these happens while it is in draft or discussing and nothing else blocks it:
   - the last open question blocking it is answered;
   - every child spec under it is done;
   - Claude's progress comments ("Reqs 1-n done") cover every requirement.
   An automatic approval is logged with actor "auto" and its reason ("all questions answered", "all child specs done", "all requirements met").
3. **Design approval questions.** When a spec needs a design, Claude asks for its approval as a question marked `design: true` (via `spill_ask`), linked to the Paper or Figma board and blocking the spec. The card shows a "Design" tag and an "Open design" link. Options: "A. Approve this design" and "B. Needs changes" (free text is a redirect: Claude revises the design and reopens the question). Approving the design closes the question and, since a design question is treated as the spec's last sign-off, auto-approves the spec (reason "design approved") even if other D-11 conditions are not met, as long as no other open question blocks it.
4. On an approved card the Approve button becomes **Build**. Pressing it sets the spec `queued` ("Queued to build").
5. Reject stays next to Build on approved and queued cards, so you can still say no after approval.
6. Claude builds `queued` specs, one at a time, in the order you pressed Build. `approved` alone no longer starts a build.
7. The card and side sheet show who or what approved it ("Approved automatically: all questions answered", "Approved by you").
8. `spill build S-n` does the same from the terminal; `spill approve` keeps working.
9. The Spill rules (MCP instructions, SPILL.md, CLAUDE.md) change from "approval means build" to "Build means build"; the inbox shows Build as `approved -> queued`.
10. The Pending approval group lists only specs not yet approved; approved specs waiting for Build show as their own "Ready to build" rows, after the pending ones.

## Out of scope
- Building several specs in parallel.
- Undoing a Build press (use Revert, S-5).

## Acceptance checks
- Approve a spec: its button reads Build and Reject is still there; Claude does not start it.
- Press Build: it moves to queued and Claude starts it.
- On a board copy, each D-11 condition alone auto-approves a spec, with actor "auto" and the matching reason on the card.
- A spec still blocked by another open question is not auto-approved.
- Answer a design question with "Approve this design": the question closes and the spec becomes approved with reason "design approved".
- Reject an approved spec: it moves to rejected with the reason.

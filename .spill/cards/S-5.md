## Purpose
A spec that was approved can be taken back down, and the board shows that it was once approved and why it was pulled.

## Requirements
1. The user can revert an `approved`, `active` or `done` spec, with an optional reason.
2. A reverted card loses the green and the shimmer, shows a "Reverted" label with a strike through the old seal, and the reason on the card.
3. If the spec was `active`, Claude is notified through `spill_inbox` and must stop work on it.
4. The card history keeps the approval and the revert (who, when, reason).
5. A reverted spec can be edited and sent for approval again.

## Out of scope
- Automatically reverting code that implemented the spec.

## Acceptance checks
- Revert an active spec: working ring stops, `spill_inbox` reports the revert, card shows reason.

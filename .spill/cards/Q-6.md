Three spec viewer designs are in Paper (page Work board v2, boards 10, 10A and 10B). Claude recommends B: it keeps the board in view, which is the point of Spill, and opening a card stays a light action.

Options:
- A. Popup (board 10): centered modal, body left and facts right, most information at once; covers the canvas
- B. Side sheet (board 10A): docks right, canvas stays live, tabs for Overview / Links / Activity / File
- C. Document reader (board 10B): full page like the markdown file, comments pinned to the line they are about

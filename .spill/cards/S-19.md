## Purpose
A rejection is the user telling Claude to rework something. Claude should never have to dig through the inbox or the event log to find one: rejections wait in their own queue until Claude deals with them.

## Requirements
1. Every rejection (from the CLI or the app) enters a rejection queue with the card, the user's reason, and the time.
2. A new MCP tool `spill_rejections` lists the waiting items, oldest first, each with its card title and full reason.
3. An item leaves the queue when Claude moves the card out of `rejected` (revised and back to `discussing`), or when Claude acknowledges it with `spill_rejections` `{ resolve: ID, note }` to leave the card rejected for good. The note is added to the card as a comment.
4. Rejecting the same card again puts it back in the queue with the new reason.
5. `spill_board` and `spill_inbox` start with a line naming the waiting rejections ("Rejections waiting: S-18"), until the queue is empty.
6. The app shows waiting rejections in the bottom-left panel, above the spotlight, in red, with the reason. Clicking one pans to the card.

## Out of scope
- Rejecting questions or decisions.
- Notifications outside the app (see Q-1).

## Acceptance checks
- `spill reject S-n reason` makes `spill_rejections` list S-n with that reason, and `spill_board` starts with "Rejections waiting: S-n".
- Moving S-n to `discussing` empties the queue; resolving with a note also empties it, keeps S-n rejected, and adds the note as a comment.
- The app panel shows the item within a second of the rejection and drops it when resolved.
